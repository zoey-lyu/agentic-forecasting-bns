West Texas Intermediate (WTI) crude oil prices have experienced a dramatic, historic surge over the past week, driven by the sudden outbreak of a major military conflict in the Middle East.

Prior to the outbreak of the war, the demand outlook for 2026 was already characterized by a significant divergence between major forecasting agencies.
