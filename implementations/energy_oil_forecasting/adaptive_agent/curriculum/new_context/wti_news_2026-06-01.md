The global demand outlook for 2026 remains highly contested, with major forecasting agencies offering widely divergent projections. OPEC expects global oil demand to grow by 1.2 million bpd in 2026 (comprising 0.1 million bpd in the OECD and 1.1 million bpd in non-OECD countries) and projects a rebound of 1.54 million bpd in 2027. In contrast, the US Energy Information Administration (EIA) lowered its 2026 demand growth forecast to just 0.2 million bpd, expecting a 0.3 million bpd contraction in the OECD to be offset by a 0.5 million bpd non-OECD expansion. The International Energy Agency (IEA) is the most bearish, forecasting a global demand contraction of 420,000 bpd for 2026, driven by a 0.3 million bpd decline in the OECD and a 0.1 million bpd decline in non-OECD economies.
